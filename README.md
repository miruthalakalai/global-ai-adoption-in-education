# global-ai-adoption-in-education

## Project Overview

This project analyzes global Artificial Intelligence (AI) adoption in the education sector using data analytics and Tableau visualization. The analysis explores AI adoption patterns across countries, regions, students, teachers, and rural and urban communities.

## Objectives

- Analyze global AI adoption trends in education.
- Compare AI usage between students and teachers.
- Study country-wise and region-wise AI adoption patterns.
- Compare AI adoption between rural and urban communities.
- Analyze the market share of popular AI tools used in education.

## Dataset

The dataset contains global AI adoption records in the education sector from 2015 to 2026.

### Dataset Details

- Total Records: 1,360
- Number of Countries: 10
- Time Period: 2015–2026
- Regions Covered: 6

## Key Insights

- Average Daily AI Usage: 32.82 minutes
- Average Urban AI Usage: 33.56%
- Average Rural AI Usage: 21.60%
- High Adoption Schools: 680
- Moderate Adoption Schools: 473
- Low Adoption Schools: 207
